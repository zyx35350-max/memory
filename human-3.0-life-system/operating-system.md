# Personal Operating System

## 1. One Main Thing

Every day has one primary objective.

Everything else is secondary.

## 2. Capacity before ambition

Plan for what can actually fit.

Start below maximum capacity. Increase workload only after the routine becomes stable.

**Less, but consistently** beats **everything, then collapse**.

## 3. The 60% rule

If only about 60% of the planned work is completed:
- do not panic;
- do not create a late-night recovery session;
- identify whether the plan was oversized or unclear;
- reduce or reorder tomorrow's plan.

Repeated 60% completion means the plan is too large, not that you need more punishment.

## 4. Information firewall

For every new tool, career idea, course, model, or project, ask:

**Does this directly support the current 90-day objective?**

If no:
- save it in a backlog;
- do not act on it now.

A backlog protects curiosity without allowing curiosity to control the calendar.

## 5. Learning-to-output rule

A useful learning session should eventually create something:
- a note;
- an experiment;
- a product improvement;
- a portfolio artifact;
- a published idea;
- a better interview answer.

The unit of progress is not hours consumed.

It is **capability demonstrated**.

## 6. Weekly review

Once per week answer:
1. What created the most value?
2. What distracted me?
3. What evidence did reality give me?
4. What should I stop doing next week?
5. What is the single most important result for next week?

Do not redesign the entire life every week.

# Career stop-loss system

### Day 30
Am I actually exposed to the skills I want?

### Day 90
Do I have measurable project evidence?

### Month 6
Has my market value increased?

If repeated evidence says no, prepare the next move.

**Stop-loss does not mean quitting impulsively.** It means refusing to stay indefinitely in a low-growth environment.

# Decision rule

When two options compete, favor the option that increases some combination of:

**transferable skill + future optionality + strong people/network exposure + market feedback + autonomy**

rather than optimizing only for immediate comfort or salary.
